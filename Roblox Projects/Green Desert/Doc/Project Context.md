Game Name: Green Desert



Genre: Incremental



Target Audience: 6-22





Platform: PC and Mobile





Art Style: Simple and detailed 





Currencies: Gold coins





Core Gameplay:

1\. Explore the world.

2\. Find a water source.

3\. Refill your water tool.

4\. Select a tool.

5\. Spray or drop water onto the environment.

6\. Grow grass, plants and trees.

7\. Restore the surrounding area.

8\. Repeat in other areas.

Transform a barren world into a lush, thriving environment using different water tools and environmental restoration mechanics.
